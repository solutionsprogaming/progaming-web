Privacy policy

How we handle the information of people who visit progamingsolutions.net and contact us.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

ProGaming Solutions ("we") provides technology for online gaming operators: custom platforms, credit sales, API integrations and built-in chat. We are responsible for processing the data described in this policy. You can write to progamingsolutions@gmail.com with any question about your data.

2. What information we collect

  • Data you send us: when you message us on WhatsApp, Telegram or email, we receive your name, number or username, and whatever you tell us in your message (for example, the service you are interested in or the name of your platform). This site has no forms that store data on our servers: the buttons open the app you choose with the message already written.
  • Browsing data: pages visited, buttons you click, device and browser type, approximate IP address and the page you came from.

3. Cookies and measurement tools

  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms): measures visits and clicks on the WhatsApp buttons so we know which ads work and can show ads to interested people. It uses cookies and may link the visit to your Facebook or Instagram account under Meta's policy.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and logs technical data (such as the IP address) to deliver the pages and protect the site against attacks.
  • Google Fonts: loads the site's typefaces; your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage the ads you see in your Meta account's ad settings.

4. How we use the information

  • To answer your questions and send you the information, demos or proposals you request.
  • For sales follow-up and customer support.
  • To measure site usage and the results of our campaigns in order to improve them.
  • To protect the site and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Who we share it with

Only with the providers we need to operate: messaging services (WhatsApp, Telegram, email), our customer management tool, Cloudflare and Meta. Each processes the data under its own privacy policy. We may also share it when a competent authority requires it by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your contact data while there is a business relationship or an active conversation, and afterwards for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or delete your data, and withdraw your permission for us to contact you. Write to progamingsolutions@gmail.com and we will reply within a reasonable time. Depending on your country, you can also contact the relevant data protection authority.

8. Adults only

Our services are intended for businesses and people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the current version here with its update date.
