How to start an online casino in 2026: steps and what you need

From choosing a business model to welcoming your first players: what you need to sort out, in order, to launch your platform.

ProGaming Solutions · October 29, 2026 · 5 min read

Launching an online casino no longer requires an in-house development team or months of work. What it does require is a few decisions made in the right order. These are the steps we go through with every operator who launches a platform with us.

1. Define your operating model

The first question is how you will reach your players. In Latin America the most common model is agents and cashiers: a network of people who load credit for players and support them on WhatsApp or Telegram. Other operators prefer an open site with direct deposits. Your model defines which levels your admin panel needs and how you will get paid.

2. Check the regulation in your market

Every country, and sometimes every province or state, has its own rules for online gaming. Before investing, confirm with a local advisor which license or permits you need and what is required for age verification and anti-money-laundering. Gaming is for adults 18+ only, and each operator is responsible for complying with the regulation of its market.

3. Choose a brand and domain

Your brand is what players remember and recommend. Pick a short name that is easy to type and has an available domain. Prepare a high-quality logo and two or three colors; that is enough to build the identity of the whole platform.

4. Choose the platform

You have two options: build from scratch or use a ready platform customized with your brand. The second is faster and cheaper, and lets you focus on getting players. Before deciding, check that it includes:

  • A multi-level admin panel for agents, cashiers and players.
  • A wide catalog of games from well-known providers.
  • Reports on transactions and commissions.
  • Real technical support, every day.

At ProGaming Solutions we deliver your custom platform with your brand in 1 week.

5. Add the games

Players look for titles they already know: popular slots, roulette and live casino. With a games API you integrate thousands of titles from several providers at once, instead of negotiating and integrating them one by one.

6. Set up payments and support

Decide how your players will load and withdraw credit and who will support them. If you work with cashiers, give them a clear process and ready-made answers. And don't depend on a single channel: when a WhatsApp number goes down, sales stop. A chat built into the platform keeps you in touch with your players no matter what.

7. Launch and measure

Start with a small group of agents, see which games are played the most and adjust. Track how many active players you have each week and how much each agent loads: those two numbers tell you whether the operation is growing.
