How to start selling online casino credits as an agent or cashier

The agent and cashier model, step by step: choose a platform, buy credits, support your players and grow without depending on one number.

ProGaming Solutions · October 30, 2026 · 5 min read

In much of Latin America, online gaming runs on a network of agents and cashiers: people who buy credits from the platform and load them for their players, whom they support on WhatsApp or Telegram. It is a model that lets you start without building anything yourself. These are the steps to get off to a good start.

What a cashier does and what an agent does

  • Cashier: creates accounts for their players, loads credits when they pay and processes their withdrawals.
  • Agent: besides supporting players, manages cashiers or sub-agents and sells credits to them.

Many start as cashiers and become agents once their customer base grows.

1. Choose the platform

Your players will play wherever you load them, so the platform is your product. Check that it has well-known games, works well on mobile and has an agent panel that is easy to use. At ProGaming Solutions we work with 11 platforms ready to operate, and we help you choose the one that best fits your audience.

2. Buy your first credits

Start with an amount you can move in a few days: it is better to top up often than to sit on idle credits. The higher the volume, the better the price per credit. With us you can pay in Argentine pesos or USDT, and the credits are added to your agent account.

3. Set up your support channel

Fast support is what makes a player load with you again instead of someone else. Have ready-made answers to the usual questions: how to load, how to withdraw, how long it takes. And don't depend on a single number: add a Telegram username as a backup and, if you can, a chat inside the platform.

4. Keep track of every transaction

Record every load and every withdrawal with date, player and amount. The agent panel shows the history, but keeping your own record avoids disputes and shows you who your most active players are.

5. Pay withdrawals quickly

Nothing builds trust like a withdrawal paid on time. A player who gets paid fast recommends their cashier; one who waits leaves and tells others why.

6. Grow with sub-agents and referrals

Once your operation is in order, you can add cashiers under you and offer a perk to players who bring friends. That way you grow without handling every conversation yourself.

Mistakes that cost sales

  • Working with a single WhatsApp number: if it gets blocked, top-ups stop.
  • Running out of credits during peak hours.
  • Promising players winnings: gaming is entertainment and no one can guarantee results.
  • Not checking your country's rules or the legal age: gaming is for adults 18+ only, and each operator is responsible for complying with the regulation of its market.
